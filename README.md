# Adventurer's Indemnity

> Kahramanlar zindandan döndü. Faturayı sana kesiyorlar.

**Adventurer's Indemnity**, fantastik bir krallıkta geçen kara mizah tonunda bir sigorta yönetim oyunudur. Zindandan dönen maceracıların hasar taleplerini inceler, kanıtları değerlendirir ve ödeme ya da ret kararı verirsiniz. Kararlarınız şirketin ekonomisini, itibarını ve maceracıların kaderini etkiler.

10 günlük demoda büronu yönet, maceracılarla sözleşme yap ve geri dönen hasar dosyalarını incele.

## English

**Adventurer's Indemnity** is a darkly comic insurance management game set in a fantasy kingdom. Review adventurers' claims, investigate the evidence, and decide whether to pay or deny each case. Your decisions affect the company's finances, its reputation, and the adventurers' fates.

Play a ten-day demo in Turkish or English: negotiate contracts, inspect damaged equipment and decide which claims to pay.

The 26 October package includes the updated office scene, new desk and room materials, window views and current item-inspection placement. It retains the ten-day demo, Turkish / English interface and four selectable music tracks. Download size: approximately 385 MB (RAR).

Known limitations: the lower part of the window view can stretch, and some item models and parts are still in development.

Version 0.33.15 places hammer-head damage on the metal head and orb-rim cracks on the metal support band. The glass sphere remains intact in the rim-crack case. Damage placement uses the actual parts of the existing models.

Also includes the restored animated doorway portal, with customer arrivals and departures bound to the visible door surface. Pick handle cuts now shorten the handle across its own axis; sword handles retain the attached end.

Also includes the fixes for first-day decision stamps when guidance progress lags behind, overlapping parchment numerals, newspaper image alignment and staff crack selection. The report footer has been removed from claim forms.

Read the newspaper over the live office and scroll the entire page with the mouse wheel. Finished jobs leave an empty desk; **My Contracts** keeps existing policies accessible. **F10** hides or restores the UI for screenshots; **Esc** exits screenshot mode.

Waiting new contracts appear as closed scrolls on the desk. Click a scroll to open its negotiation. The existing doorway light was repositioned and aimed to cast character shadows onto the floor.

The main menu includes the official Tusvaren Games studio logo. Natural recordings replace the seal, paper, coin, wood, door, day-end bell and footstep effects. Footsteps follow actual movement; adjust the volume in Settings.

Choose from four CC0 music tracks in Settings. Master, effects and music levels are independent, and the selection is saved. Set music to zero to mute it. The recordings by RandomMind allow commercial use without mandatory attribution.
